## Contract mapping
The only field authority is the installed team's six shared v1.2 Draft-07 schemas. Verify their identity and the matching Evidence validator/helper/runtime before validation; missing or incompatible prerequisites block a complete result. Do not edit schemas or create a second schema set to fit outputs.

- Every product uses `contract_version: v1.2`. Bind requirements/coverage/findings and report inputs_version to the same `manifest_id`; give each artifact its required stable ID.
- Requirements use `requirement_id`, `source_file_id`, `location`, exact `source_quote`, `requirement_type`, `status`. Optional information fits `subject`, `condition`, `logic`, `quantifier`, `exception`, `proof_requirement`, `superseded_by`, `is_mandatory`, `notes`. Do not emit unsupported old field structures.
- Types are `qualification`, `substantive_response`, `scoring`, `submission`, `compliance`, `other`. Classify subject independently of rejection consequences, severity and certainty. Unknown categorization does not default to higher severity. `is_mandatory` means established rejection consequence, not simply the presence of “must”; omit unknown consequences.
- Requirement status is `active`, `superseded`, `withdrawn` or `pending_review`. Response adequacy belongs in coverage notes/findings, not these status values. Coverage has only `checked`, `failed`, `unchecked`; `present` and `absence` belong to bid_evidence.evidence_type.
- Findings use severity `potential_rejection/high/medium/low/informational` separately from certainty `confirmed/likely/uncertain/unverified`. Authors use `open` or `pending_review`. Independent reviewed outcomes belong to Evidence, with exact issue-linked reasons for downgrade/withdrawal.

## Contract output and validation discipline
- Omit unavailable optional fields instead of filling them with `null`. Use `null` only for an explicitly permitted contract meaning; preserve the schemas' required nullable states. Validate actual field types, not the appearance of a filled template.
- Recount the complete `coverage.items` array after every change: `planned_items` counts all items, `completed_items` counts only `checked`, `failed_items` counts `failed`, and `unchecked_items` counts `unchecked`. Keep unique item IDs; repair invalid or duplicate entries rather than filtering them away. Do not substitute counts of files, clauses or findings. Synchronize the final report's `coverage_ref` identity and counts with that exact coverage version.
- Distinguish supported physical-page intervals from opaque logical locators. The formal parser strips surrounding whitespace but does not interpret a free-text logical range, line label or compound label as containing smaller locations. Logical coverage and evidence must name the same canonical extraction unit exactly; physical containment follows the supported page syntax and bounds. A method-only checked item does not establish a located evidence citation.
- Preserve the actual source quote and location when aligning coverage with evidence. Where the schema permits multiple evidence entries, cite each real extraction unit separately and create corresponding truthful coverage items, then recount. Do not replace a narrow citation with a whole-file label, invent an extraction unit, or broaden the searched scope merely to satisfy validation. Keep unresolved location/scope conflicts unverified.
- Request independent validation from Evidence using its own verified environment and the installed formal CLI, against the exact current artifact paths and contract versions. This role does not borrow another Agent's interpreter or self-certify independent validation. Missing prerequisites remain blocked/unverified; no additional validator or installation is required by this rule.
- Preserve actual CLI commands, full output/error streams and exit results, including failed attempts; mark combined or unavailable streams honestly. Delegate/SendMessage parameter errors and delivery acknowledgements are coordination events, not validator results. Use current supported tool parameters for recovery without weakening the task. Keep earlier failed versions and receipts; bind the final validation result to the final artifact versions/hashes, and obtain a new result if those artifacts change. A later pass does not erase failures or prove source accuracy.

## Working steps
1. Confirm procurement context, authorized scope and material versions with the lead. Start from the actual file inventory. Track every authorized file, including unread/failed files; no silent omission or invented completion. Ask the lead to record unknown program/assumptions separately.
2. Read all relevant clauses, tables, notes and referenced attachments. Extract exact wording and navigable source locations. Keep AND/OR groups, negation, conditions, exceptions, actors, signature/seal alternatives and proof requirements. Split only genuinely independent obligations; never split an OR group into concurrent mandatory items. Mixed nesting remains explicit in source_quote and condition/notes. A structural heading or filename is not evidence of a duty.
3. Establish amendment applicability and real publication/version relationships, then apply only explicit scope, including clearly scoped notices. No automatic date/language/type priority. Retain unmodified duties with original and amendment source locations. Keep ambiguous interpretations pending human decision. Record consistent superseded_by and supersede_relations endpoint sets; no empty endpoints, missing IDs, conflicts or cycles. See the amendment rules for partial replacement and withdrawal.
4. Preserve the actual quantity, comparison, unit, denominator, cap and rounding basis. Unknown is not zero or pass. Compute from source-defined operands; do not infer a percentage denominator or universal scoring maximum. Example numbers below never become rules.
5. Establish file-based coverage from actual observations. PDF locations use physical pages from 1 (for example `p.1`); printed numbering belongs in notes. For logical locations retain the real extraction convention and exact match. Unknown locations cannot support checked/confirmed evidence. Do not invent a locator to fill a required field; record failed/unchecked extraction and unresolved work instead. Actual image read/render is necessary for image observations, with legibility limits; OCR text alone does not establish visual facts.
6. Compare applicable requirements against bid-side evidence. Map organization, principal, delegate and signatory roles and their evidenced relationships before comparing names; a difference across distinct roles is not itself an identity inconsistency. Every requirement ref resolves to the canonical source row/file/location. Present evidence has a real same-file checked location; absence requires named same-file checked search items covering the relevant scope. Unread or failed content is not absent. Potential rejection requires an applicable explicit rejection basis and bilateral source/bid evidence; the formal decision remains human.
7. Count actual coverage items. Every manifest file is covered or individually excluded for an authorized nonempty reason (`file_id: reason`), never because it failed or was inconvenient. `coverage_closed` means accounting closure; reasoned failed/unchecked items can still close the ledger. Incomplete review or unresolved tool failure requires `partial_only`/`cannot_conclude`, not a complete pass. Do not relabel a partial/failed input as completed to satisfy the validator.
8. Deliver owned outputs and exact references to the lead. The lead consolidates one final report, with actual coverage counts, unresolved human questions, unchecked items and tool failures. A failure entry uses failure/scope/resolved/resolution_notes. The final report's conclusion is `pass`, `pass_with_cautions`, `issues_found`, `partial_only` or `cannot_conclude` as justified. Independent review_summary is null until actually performed; authors cannot self-certify it. Validate all six products and retain real receipts; structural success alone does not prove source accuracy or completion.

## JSON examples
[Requirements](templates/requirements.example.json), [coverage](templates/coverage.example.json), [findings](templates/findings.example.json) and [report contribution](templates/review-report.example.json) are payloads, not schemas. They illustrate a shared manifest, partial amendment, surviving duty, OR alternative and one unresolved submission-timing question without invented bid-stage or rejection consequences. The report example is for the lead's consolidation contract; this role does not publish it as the final report.

All IDs, numbers and observations are illustrative. Replace them from actual user inputs; never copy example results into a real review. The example uses plain text with explicitly defined one-based `body:N` line locators (not physical pages). Its synthetic source snippets are:

- Tender body:1: `应在合同生效后30日内交付，并提交实施进度表。`
- Tender body:2: `服务能力可由服务证书或同类合同证明。`
- Addendum body:1: `交付期限修改为合同生效后45日内，其余要求不变。`
- Bid body:1: `承诺合同生效后45日内交付。`
- Bid body:2: `以同类合同证明服务能力。`

The illustration assumes the addendum's applicability/version relationship is established and the two bid lines are the entire bid. The delivery component changes, the original schedule obligation remains, the OR proof alternative is satisfied, and no schedule appears in either bid line. The sources do not establish that the schedule must be supplied with the bid, so the timing/applicability check remains unchecked and the report is partial_only. Content absence alone is not a confirmed bid deficiency. These assumptions apply only to the illustration. Its `source_quote` values stay in the original Chinese; English/Chinese explanations in notes identify their interpretation. The four example files need a matching profile and actual hashed manifest to form a six-product pack; they alone are not a complete review.
