# Requirement extraction

Work within the user's assigned scope. Read relevant clauses, tables, footnotes and referenced appendices. A heading alone is not a duty. Keep each independently verifiable obligation whole: preserve AND/OR grouping, negation, conditions, exceptions, units and proof alternatives. Separate duties only when their logic remains intact.

Compare applicable obligations against actual bid responses. Preserve exact necessary quotations and navigable sources; explain interpretation separately. Keep actual operands, denominators, thresholds and rounding bases; unknown is not zero. Preserve signing actor, method and alternatives without interpreting image presence as authenticity.

Before reporting absence, search the relevant response sections and explain that scope. Account for unread or failed materials; they cannot establish absence. Deliver requirements, response gaps, practical actions and limitations in an appropriate format. Independent Evidence challenges the conclusions; the lead consolidates the result.
