# Requirement classification

Distinguish qualification, substantive response, scoring, submission and applicable compliance duties when useful. Classification describes subject, not severity. Preserve independent duties and compound logic. Explain unfamiliar or ambiguous categories rather than forcing a label.

Assess consequence from applicable source wording. A general “must” or missing certificate alone does not establish rejection. Scoring may include an explicit minimum threshold: neither assume all score losses are harmless nor all imply rejection. Separate business impact from confidence in the evidence.

Prioritize credible serious risks without inflating severity to compensate for uncertainty. Formal decisions stay with authorized people; independently reviewed status requires actual independent opinion, not self-certification.
