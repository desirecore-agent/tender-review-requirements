# Amendment handling

Confirm applicability, publication/version relationships and explicit scope. A label, date or language alone does not determine precedence. Read the original and relevant amendments; identify replacement, partial modification, clarification, withdrawal or new duties. Preserve all unaffected obligations and cite both original and amendment sources.

Track the effective interpretation in clear prose or a table. A full replacement retains traceability to the old duty; a partial change does not retire the whole clause. Never manufacture a combined quotation. Conflicting authority or language stays unresolved with candidate interpretations and a concrete clarification request. A bid mentioning old and new values needs consistency review, not an automatic pass.
